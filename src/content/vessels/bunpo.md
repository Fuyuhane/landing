## The Vessel

When a bee colony grows strong enough, part of it leaves to begin life elsewhere. Bunpo borrows that image of purposeful distribution, not to make medicine sentimental, but to remember that every donation belongs to a larger living network. Its first useful form is modest: help donors remember their own history, understand when they may be able to give again, and see careful signals of regional need.

## Core Principles

- **Privacy first**; health data minimized, consented, and audited
- **Impact with restraint**; meaningful feedback without exposing a recipient
- **Graceful scope**; each approved integration level must stand on its own
- **Medical boundaries**; coordination, not clinical decisioning
- **France first**; legal, HDS provider, CNIL, and partnership gates come before launch

## Operational Capabilities

| Capability | Engine |
|------------|--------|
| Donor coordination | Profiles, manual donation history, eligibility windows, reminders |
| Regional awareness | EFS/ARS/Santé publique signals or curated indicators by region |
| Approved partner path | Verified records and need broadcasting only after authority agreements |
| Impact connection | Delayed, coarse, privacy-reduced updates after partner and DPIA review |
| Emergency mobilization | Targeted alerts issued by authorized organizations |
| Launch gates | France-first legal review, HDS provider, privacy, security, and retention controls |
